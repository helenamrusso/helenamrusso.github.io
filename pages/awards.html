Grants

  • 2026K99/R00 Pathway to Independence Award, National Institute of Neurological Disorders and Stroke (NINDS/NIH)

    Impact of microbial metabolites and their roles in HIV-associated neurocognitive disorders · Principal Investigator · 2026–2031

    Despite effective antiretroviral therapy, nearly half of people with HIV experience comorbidities such as HIV-associated neurocognitive disorders (HAND). This project integrates metabolomics, metagenomics, microbial culturing, and in vivo and in vitro models to identify how HIV- and therapy-modulated microbial metabolites, particularly N-acyl lipids and bile acids, contribute to neuroinflammation and neurocognitive impairment. It will also deliver MS/MS spectral libraries and a publicly accessible atlas of microbial metabolites associated with HIV and HAND.
